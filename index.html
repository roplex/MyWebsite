01 / About

Geospatial engineering at the intersection of climate and impact

My work uses satellite intelligence to support the people and places most exposed to environmental risk. I'm based in Nairobi, working with international partners remotely and with communities and stakeholders in the field. I'm open to collaborations in Earth Observation, climate risk intelligence, conservation GIS and humanitarian geospatial analysis across Sub-Saharan Africa and beyond.

ROXR Earth · 2026 – present · Flagship

ROXR Climate Intelligence Index (RCII)

A satellite-driven drought intelligence and forecasting system for arid and semi-arid land (ASAL) regions. A six-layer Python pipeline ingests MODIS vegetation and land surface temperature imagery alongside CHIRPS rainfall, measures anomalies against a 2000–2015 baseline, and fuses them into a single index normalised by county and season.

Three Random Forest models trained on 26 years of history forecast drought severity 30, 60 and 90 days ahead, validated against Kenya's documented 2011, 2017 and 2022 droughts. Every run is version-controlled and logged for audit and replay.

Each month the system produces a PDF intelligence brief, an interactive Folium dashboard and JSON, CSV and GeoJSON data feeds for agricultural insurers, development finance institutions and humanitarian agencies.
